ADVOCACY AGAINST SCHOOL VIOLENCE

Building a safer school starts with respect, kindness, awareness, and action.

What approaches should we do to lessen school violence?

To build a place where children are safe and comfortable we must

01

Teach them to speak up

We should teach students to have confidence, to speak up against bullying and school violence. For that to happen we should teach students to tell adults, and have trust in themselves to do that.

  • Help them build confidence to report bullying
  • Teach them expressing their feelings are alright, and something that shouldn't be taken lightly.
  • Support victims, and speak up against violence.
02

Set clear rules

Establish clear school rules and consequences to prevent violence and ensure that everyone feels safe and respected. We must also put visible posters clearly stating these concerns.

03

Have a way to promote kindness

To promote kindness and positivity we must conduct activities that will help students build relationships and know each other and treat others with compassion.

  • Through activities, students will be taught how to socialize with others with fun activities.
  • Students can create posters, arts, and many things that can promote advocacy against violence.
  • This will teach students to help, and include their classmates in actitivities.
04

Be someone who acts to make things right

Encourage students to take positive action when they witness violence or injustice and help create a safer school environment.

  • Report incidents of violence to teachers or school authorities.
  • Offer support to students who are being bullied.
  • Help resolve conflicts peacefully without putting yourself in danger.
05

Promote respect and empathy

Teach students to respect each other and understand the feelings of others.

  • Respect where each other came from such as backgrounds, and difference.
  • Listen to others and try to understand their feelings.
  • Be someone who'll help others in need.

Learn to be kind.
